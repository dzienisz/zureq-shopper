ZUREQShopper

Welcome to ZUREQ Shopper. Get started in three quick steps.

  1. Connect with ZUREQ

    Sign in with your ZUREQ account (Google works). Searches use your own ZUREQ credits.

    Use an API key instead

  2. Open the side panel

    Search independent shops, build lists, and watch products without leaving your current page.

    Click the ZUREQ icon in the toolbar

  3. Search with ZUREQ

    Select text on any page, right-click it, then choose Search Zureq for “your selection”.
